Analysis

Key findings

Context & watch3
Context

2025 was the peak year

Salary-deduct sales rose from ₱279K (Sep–Dec 2023) to ₱4.44M in 2024 and ₱7.57M in 2025, almost all pork. At the current pace, 2026 will close near ₱2.81M, about 63% below 2025.

Watch

April–May mixed EFSP and regular salary deduct

EFSP was of April sales and 69.3% of May sales. Regular salary deduction ran alongside it because MMFI reopened it for MMFI employees only, due to minimal EFSP orders. Across Apr–Sep, EFSP totaled and 2,443 kg.

Watch

EFSP moved orders rather than adding them

Total salary-deduct sales averaged ₱288K a month in April–May, 6% below the Jan–Mar average of ₱305K. Over the same months regular deduction fell from ₱305K to ₱75K a month, so most EFSP orders replaced purchases that would otherwise have gone through regular deduction.

Concerns4
Concern

EFSP did not narrow the gap to 2025

The smallest gaps to 2025 were in January (−54%) and February (−51%), before EFSP started. Jan–Mar combined was 58% below 2025. April–May, with EFSP, was 60% below. From June to September the gap widened to 69–77%.

Concern

EFSP orders fell sharply after May

No EFSP purchases were recorded in June and July. August and September were about ₱21–22K each, roughly a tenth of April–May. Beef has had no EFSP sales since May. Total sales in June–July averaged ₱156K a month, 49% below Jan–Mar.

Concern

Overall salary-deduct sales are down sharply

Jan–Sep 2026 totals ₱2.10M, 65% below the same period of 2025 (₱5.95M). Pork value is down 76%. Neither channel has reversed this decline.

Concern

Up to 13 days to receive an EFSP order

EFSP needs 10 days of gathering, consolidation, preparation and document checks before release, then a 3-day releasing period. Regular salary deduct is a walk-in: employees order at MMFI and take the order home the same day. The longer wait is a likely reason employees moved back to regular deduction once it reopened.

Positive1
Positive

Higher beef share in EFSP

Beef was 20% of EFSP sales value, compared with 10% of regular salary deduct in 2026.

Recommendation

Resume regular salary deduction for all business units

Reopen walk-in regular salary deduction at the MMF Meat Cutting Plant (MCP) for every business unit, using a set weekly schedule, an eligibility check at the counter and fixed payroll cut-offs.

Why resume

What the data and the process show

₱305K

Average monthly sales in Jan–Mar 2026, when only regular salary deduction ran. This was the highest level of 2026.

Same day

Regular deduction releases the order on the same visit. EFSP takes up to 13 days from order gathering to the end of the releasing period.

Minimal

EFSP orders were low enough that MMFI already reopened regular deduction for MMFI employees. No EFSP purchases were recorded in June–July.

−51% to −54%

January and February had the smallest gaps to 2025, both before EFSP started.

Proposed process

Regular salary deduction, step by step

Steps 2–6 happen while the employee is at the counter, and the order goes home the same day. Steps 7–11 are back-office steps that record and bill the sale.

HRSales staffMCP personnelSML approverAccounting
Before the cut-off
  1. HRSend the updated employee list per cut-off.
At the counterSame visit
  1. Sales staffVerify that the employee is eligible for meat cuts salary deduction. Not eligible → tell the employee they may buy the items in cash.
  2. Sales staffReceive the order and forward it to MCP personnel. Item not available → tell the employee so they can change or cancel the order.
  3. Sales staffGive the employee the Authority to Deduct (ATD) form to fill in and sign.
  4. MCP personnelPrepare the order, the delivery receipt (DR) and the dispatch form.
  5. MCP personnelRelease the order together with the signed DR.Employee leaves with the order.
Back office
  1. Sales staffCreate the sales order (SO) in the system.
  2. SML approverApprove the system SO.
  3. Sales staffForward the approved SO to MCP.
  4. MCP personnelFulfill the SO.
  5. AccountingBill the SO.
Keep the summarized SO, with attachments. Each summarized SO carries the duplicate copies of the DRs, the original ATD forms and the dispatch forms, so Accounting can bill and deduct from one complete set.
